## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the delicate intersection of botanical romanticism and editorial high fashion. Rooted in an aesthetic evocative of Monet's water lilies translated into modern bespoke luxury, the design evokes serenity, timeless refinement, tactile opulence, and intimate exclusivity.

Targeted at discerning clientele within luxury lifestyle, high-end bridal, fine botanical perfumery, and prestige couture spaces, the aesthetic marries:
- **Haute Editorial Minimalism:** Generous breathing room, deliberate negative space, and commanding high-contrast serif headlines.
- **Organic Tactility:** Soft, warm, parchment-like surfaces balanced with deep verdant shadows and delicate floral blush accents.
- **Refined Craftsmanship:** Hairline borders, subtle ambient warmth, and tailored component finishes that feel hand-crafted rather than industrial.

## Colors

The palette directly draws from the impressionistic water lily sanctuary in the reference:

- **Primary (`#D3968C` / Rosy Brown):** The focal blossom. Used deliberately for primary interactive elements, crucial calls-to-action, romantic flourishes, delicate badges, and key selection states. It brings human warmth and floral grace to the composition.
- **Secondary (`#0A3323` / Dark Green):** The deep pond depths. Serves as the bedrock of visual hierarchy: principal text typography, authoritative navigation elements, rich borders, and high-impact editorial moments.
- **Tertiary (`#105666` / Midnight Green):** The crystalline shadowed water. Applied to secondary interactive tiers, specialized tags, contextual pill badges, and supplementary graphic accents.
- **Neutral (`#F7F4D5` / Warm Alabaster Beige):** The canvas. Forms the luminous, organic base for page backgrounds, card surfaces, and tactile backdrops, avoiding sterile whites in favor of warm, luxurious linen-like clarity.
- **Accent Highlight (`#839958` / Moss Green):** The floating pad and vegetative flora. Deployed for subtle outline borders, botanical tags, metadata indicators, and tertiary micro-interactions.

## Typography

The typographical architecture juxtaposes the regal, high-contrast strokes of **Playfair Display** with the precision, legibility, and architectural clarity of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys literary sophistication and luxury editorial cadence. Headlines should lean into italicised styles for standalone key phrases or pull quotes to accent bespoke craftsmanship.
- **Functional Body & Interfaces (Manrope):** Ensures effortless readability across dense information, product specifications, forms, and micro-copy. 
- **Labels & Micro-Details:** Always set with generous tracking (`0.05em` to `0.12em`), frequently styled in uppercase for categorized headers, metadata, and prestige badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy favors an expansive, unhurried 12-column fluid grid system across desktop, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a focused 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Negative Space as Luxury:** Generous outer margins (`margin: 3rem`) and ample vertical section spacing (`space-2xl: 4rem`) safeguard the content from visual noise, reinforcing high-tier catalog presentation.
- **Asymmetry & Editorial Offsets:** Hero and product storytelling layouts incorporate staggered card heights and intentional alignment shifts to mimic print magazine layouts.
- **Responsive Adaptability:** On mobile viewports, gutters compact to `1rem` and margins taper to `1.25rem` while preserving inner component padding to avoid an overcrowded feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface stacking and ethereal, ambient diffusion rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas rests on `#F7F4D5`. Elevated surfaces—such as cards, drawers, and modal sheets—use an immaculate parchment tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`) or pure ivory `#FFFFFF` overlaid on top of the beige canvas.
- **Botanical Ambient Glow:** Drop shadows are ultra-diffused, using deep forest pigments rather than gray. Shadows apply `#0A3323` at 4% to 8% opacity with high blur radii (e.g., `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 51, 35, 0.06)`).
- **Hairline Contouring:** Components use gossamer-thin 1px borders colored in translucent moss `#839958` at 25% to 35% opacity or muted dark green at 12% opacity, delivering crisp delineation without visual weight.
- **Frosted Veil:** Floating navigation headers and elevated toolbars leverage backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with `rgba(247, 244, 213, 0.85)` fill.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a curated, softened geometry (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Cards, Buttons):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius balances modern structure with touchable warmth.
- **Prominent Surfaces & Feature Cards:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) provides soft, welcoming corners reminiscent of polished river stones.
- **Full Enclosures & Modals:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for expansive hero cards and bottom sheets.
- **Badges, Chips & Floating CTAs:** Pill-shaped radius (`9999px`) to echo organic botanical petals and natural water droplets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Rosy Brown (`#D3968C`) fill with dark green (`#0A3323`) text or crisp ivory text, subtle pill or 8px rounded corners, with smooth transitions to a deepened terracotta rose on hover (`#C48479`).
- **Secondary Action:** Midnight Green (`#105666`) fill with `#F7F4D5` text, or transparent background with a 1.5px `#0A3323` hairline outline and deep green text.
- **Ghost / Editorial Link:** Underlined serif link with 4px offset in `#0A3323`, featuring an animated expanding line in `#D3968C` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges:** Uppercase tracked `label-sm` font, encapsulated within a full pill border of Moss Green (`#839958`) at 40% opacity, with a soft `#839958` 10% tint fill and `#0A3323` text.
- **Filter Chips:** Beige or ivory background with a 1px border. Selected state swaps to `#0A3323` fill with `#F7F4D5` text and a subtle warm rose glow.

### Cards & Containers
- Cards feature 16px rounded corners, rendered in soft white or ivory over the `#F7F4D5` ground. 
- Bound by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(131, 153, 88, 0.25)`).
- Hover interactions feature a gentle lift with a rosy brown bottom accent or subtle expansion of the ambient shadow.

### Input Fields
- Understated input boxes with a faint background of `#FFFFFF` with 60% opacity, framed by a 1px border of `#839958` at 30% opacity.
- Focus state activates an elegant transition to a `#105666` or `#0A3323` border with an ambient 3px glow of `#D3968C` at 20% opacity.
- Floating labels set in Manrope `label-md` with tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square with 4px rounding (checkbox) or circular ring (radio).
- Default border in `#839958`.
- Checked state fills with `#0A3323` and reveals an ivory or `#D3968C` glyph, delivering rich contrast.

### Lists & Dividers
- List rows separated by delicate hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(10, 51, 35, 0.08)`).
- Metadata presented with Manrope in `label-md` uppercase, while list titles leverage Playfair Display for editorial distinction.
